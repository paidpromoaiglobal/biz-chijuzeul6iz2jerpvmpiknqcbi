Our Story

Made with Passion

Ponni Indian Groceries is your go-to destination for authentic Indian food products right in the heart of Singapore. Located conveniently near the MRT at 72 Buffalo Road, we stock a wide and carefully curated selection of Indian groceries — from freshly ripened mangoes and seasonal vegetables to ready-made snacks and pantry staples imported directly from India. Our friendly and courteous staff are always on hand to help you find exactly what you need.

What sets us apart is our commitment to keeping Indian flavours accessible to the community around the clock — we are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including all public holidays. Whether you are an early morning shopper or a late-night craving chaser, Ponni Indian Groceries is here for you. With a strong rating of 4.4 out of 5 from our valued customers, we take pride in offering quality products at affordable prices and a warm, welcoming shopping experience.
